Goku Dragon Ball Super

Goku is the main protagonist of the Dragon Ball series. He is a Saiyan, a warrior race known for their fighting abilities and love of battle. Throughout the series, Goku embarks on numerous adventures, battles powerful foes, and continually strives to become stronger. His cheerful personality, unwavering determination, and strong sense of justice make him a beloved character among fans worldwide.

Goku's Transformations

Goku has several transformations that significantly increase his power levels. Some of his most notable forms include Super Saiyan, Super Saiyan God, and Ultra Instinct. Each transformation comes with its own unique abilities and visual changes, showcasing Goku's growth as a fighter throughout the series.
